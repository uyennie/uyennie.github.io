<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

Video 2.0 represented Canva's most significant video editing upgrade, introducing a new timeline editor and AI-powered Magic Video feature that generates videos from text prompts. As part of the Content Studio team, I contributed to the product rollout by designing launch templates, producing video content showcasing the new capabilities, and leading design trend research that informed our 2026 video template strategy.

<h2 id="product-launch">Product Launch</h2>

### Template Design & Production

I designed and produced video templates that demonstrated Video 2.0's enhanced capabilities, showcasing the new timeline editor and AI-powered Magic Video features. These templates served as both product demonstrations and practical starting points for users, balancing technical functionality with creative appeal. The work supported product marketing across launch channels while ensuring users had immediate, high-quality examples of what the new tools could achieve.

<h2 id="trend-forecasting">Trend Forecasting</h2>

### Design Trends 2026

Contributed to research identifying emerging video design trends for 2026, with a focus on the "Prompt Playground" trend, where pixel aesthetics meet creative AI experimentation. I explored how AI tools are reshaping creative play and digital expression, translating these insights into video template production that demonstrated this cultural shift. This research helped inform Content Studio's H1 2026 production strategy, ensuring our templates reflected how creators are actually engaging with AI-powered design tools.
