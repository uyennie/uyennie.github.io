<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

**Primary** automates global finance operations, unifying scattered bank accounts into a single platform. I redesigned their brand identity to elevate from functional fintech to premium enterprise solution by developing refined typography, sophisticated gradients, and polished UI elements that signal credibility for companies managing complex multi-currency operations.
