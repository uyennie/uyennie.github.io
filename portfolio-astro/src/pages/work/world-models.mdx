<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

A World Model is an AI system that generates visual reality in real time. Frame by frame, at the speed of human perception. Fast enough that the distinction between "live" and "generated" stops being meaningful.

This project explored how we might integrate World Models into Canva's creative workflow, enabling designers to generate and manipulate visual content in ways that weren't previously possible.

<h2 id="problem">Problem</h2>

The traditional design workflow is constrained by the assets available to creators. When designers need specific imagery, they face several challenges:

- Stock photos often don't match the exact vision
- Custom photography is expensive and time-consuming
- AI image generation tools produce static outputs that don't integrate seamlessly into design workflows

### The Opportunity

What if designers could generate exactly what they need, in real-time, directly within their creative environment?

<h2 id="research">Research</h2>

We conducted extensive research to understand the current landscape and user needs:

### Competitive Analysis

We analyzed existing AI image generation tools and identified key gaps in the market. Most tools treated image generation as a separate step from the design process, creating friction in the creative workflow.

### User Interviews

We spoke with 15 professional designers to understand their pain points:

- 87% reported spending significant time searching for the "right" stock image
- 92% wanted more control over AI-generated content
- 100% expressed interest in real-time generation capabilities

<h2 id="solution">Solution</h2>

Our solution centered on three key principles:

1. **Seamless Integration** — Generation happens within the design canvas, not in a separate tool
2. **Real-time Feedback** — Designers see results instantly as they adjust parameters
3. **Controllable Outputs** — Fine-grained control over style, composition, and content

### Design Iterations

We went through 4 major design iterations, testing each with users:

**Iteration 1:** Basic prompt-based generation
- Feedback: Users wanted more visual control

**Iteration 2:** Added style presets and reference images
- Feedback: Still felt disconnected from design workflow

**Iteration 3:** Canvas-integrated generation with layer support
- Feedback: Much better, but needed performance improvements

**Iteration 4:** Optimized real-time generation with progressive rendering
- Feedback: "This feels like magic" — User participant

<h2 id="final-design">Final Design</h2>

The final design achieved our goals of seamless, real-time, controllable generation. Key features include:

- **Canvas Integration:** Generate directly on the design canvas
- **Progressive Rendering:** See results in ~500ms, with refinement over 2-3 seconds
- **Style Transfer:** Apply styles from reference images or presets
- **Composition Controls:** Guide generation with simple sketches or masks

<h2 id="reflection">Reflection</h2>

### What Went Well

- Strong collaboration between design and engineering from day one
- User testing at every stage helped us course-correct early
- The progressive rendering approach significantly improved perceived performance

### What I'd Do Differently

- Start with more diverse user research participants
- Build a design system for AI controls earlier in the process
- Document decision rationale more thoroughly

### Impact

- Shipped to 10% of Canva Pro users in beta
- 4.2/5 average user satisfaction rating
- 23% increase in design completion rate for users with access
