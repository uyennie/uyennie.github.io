<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

A self-published essay and collection exploring digital selfhood in an age of constant connectivity. The publication examines how we cultivate identity online, the complexity of existing in digital spaces, and offers alternatives for more mindful engagement with technology. Printed on recycled 120gsm paper, the physical format itself is a deliberate choice; creating space for reflection away from screens. The bright cover and halftone imagery mirror the oversaturation of digital life while the spiral binding makes it tactile and accessible.
