<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

**Million** is a performance optimization tool that speeds up React applications by up to 70%. While they had established brand elements, the identity lacked cohesion and failed to emotionally connect with their core promise: speed. I redesigned their brand and website using vintage racing aesthetics as a metaphorical foundation, transforming a utilitarian developer tool into a playful, engaging experience that makes performance optimization exciting.
