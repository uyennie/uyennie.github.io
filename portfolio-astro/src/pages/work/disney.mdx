<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

Contributed to Canva's strategic brand partnerships by designing templates for major entertainment collaborations, with a primary focus on Disney's Marvel partnership. Created a suite of Marvel-branded templates featuring iconic characters like Spider-Man, Hulk, and the Fantastic Four, balancing strict brand guidelines with Canva's accessibility standards. The work made premium entertainment IP accessible to everyday creators while maintaining both design quality and brand integrity.
